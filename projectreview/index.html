Opening Section

This opening section introduces the topic, audience, and purpose of the guide.

By choosing a specific audience and focus, the student creates a clearer foundation for the project.

Helpful vs. Unhelpful AI Use

This section helps students compare responsible and irresponsible uses of AI.

The student explains both the benefits and risks while emphasizing that humans are responsible for what gets shared.

Chatbot Evidence

This section gives students the opportunity to test and evaluate AI-generated responses.

The student analyzes the chatbot's output, identifies strengths and weaknesses, and explains why human judgment is still necessary.

Image Generation

This section encourages students to think critically about AI-generated images and misinformation.

The student reflects on audience trust, realism, and the responsibility creators have when sharing AI-generated media.

Tips for Responsible Use

This final section synthesizes ideas from across the unit into practical advice for others.

The student connects recommendations back to earlier investigations and demonstrates evidence-based reasoning about AI use.
